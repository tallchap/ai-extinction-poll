# The Ashley Mason protocol — as actually prescribed to Ori

Source: Ori's email correspondence with Ashley E. Mason, PhD (UCSF Osher Center
for Integrative Medicine), 2019–2021. Osher Sleep Group July 30 – Aug 27, 2019
(group CBT-I, Bootzin stimulus-control lineage), plus individual sessions and a
Nov 2021 virtual booster. Extracted 2026-08 for the bedtime-gym skill. Protocol
content only.

## The prescription

- **Wake time: 7:00 AM, fixed.** The anchor of the whole program. Two alarms,
  one across the room. Morning accountability emails; she ran surprise spot
  checks ("Ori, I see you! Are you up?").
- **Bedtime: prescribed, not chosen.** Titrated weekly from sleep-diary
  efficiency. Ori's 2019 sequence: 12:25 AM → 12:35 → 12:20 → 12:10 AM.
  2021 booster: bedtime 12:00 AM / wake 7:00 AM; later she approved a
  12:30 / 7:30 option ("You aren't waking up too too early").
- **Efficiency drives titration.** Ori's weekly numbers: 79% → 79.7% → 82% →
  83% → 89%. Good weeks earned a 15-minute bump ("just the 15 minute bump
  really made a difference"). She granted one bump early "if you follow the
  rules this week."
- **Stimulus control (the Bootzin core):** "No being in bed unless you are
  (1) sleeping, or (2) having sex. That means that in the middle of the night,
  when you are awake, you cannot be in bed."
- **Screens off by 11 PM** (against a ~12:10 bedtime — roughly bedtime minus
  an hour). Wind-down earlier: "it's up to you to get your wind down time done
  earlier."
- **Worry time before dinner.** "Conflict and worry time before dinner!!
  Earlier in the day!! Write about it at 5 pm." Timeboxed constructive worry
  with a completion confirmation (Ori emailed her "worrying time is over!";
  she replied "Glad you got it in so that you don't worry tonight!").
- **Sleep diary always.** Ori kept an Excel tracker continuously from the 2019
  group — 1,375+ rows by Nov 2021. She required 7–14 days of diary before any
  booster ("Two weeks from today please send me the excel spreadsheet with
  14 days of sleep data"). Her Excel "sleep calculator" computed efficiency.
- **Never oversleep more than one day in a row** (2021 booster rule).

## Sick protocol (her words)

- "When sick, sleep when sleepy — when better, snap back into your times!"
- "You can take a couple of power naps during the day — 25 minute naps can be
  very restorative. You accrue more sleep debt when sick."
- "Extending wake time by 30–60 mins when sick is acceptable if it doesn't mess
  you up at night."
- "It is very common for people to get a cold, strep, etc., during this
  treatment. This is a long game. I think you should stick with it."

## Relapse doctrine

- "Insomnia is relapsing and remitting — and when you do the CBT-I protocol,
  you can improve on your terms!! Work the program."
- April 2020, after a health scare derailed months of sleep: Ori announced a
  reset; her entire reply was "Do it!" Ori: "Somehow telling you makes it more
  real. First day in, feels good." → Declaring the reset to someone is itself
  the intervention.
- Boosters are legitimate and she offered them standingly; Ori used one in
  Nov 2021.

## Ori's documented failure modes (what the gym exists to catch)

1. **Not holding the wake time.** Her bluntest email: "Hard to assess change
   from first to second week because you didn't stick to your wake time.
   Important to do that — otherwise you are suffering needlessly! Do it!"
2. **The gap between wind-down and lights-out.** Aug 2019: "I turned off my
   screens before 11pm. But ultimately [went] to sleep a little far past my
   bedtime." Screens-off compliance ≠ in-bed compliance — this is the bedtime
   procrastination moment, and it's the gym's core rep.
3. **Snoozing past wake when tired/sick.** "I keep finding myself waking up at
   my typical wake up time, but being sleepy enough to snooze for another
   1–2 hours."
4. **Schedule collapse under disruption.** Travel, overnights at family's,
   life events: "Since I moved my bedtime up about a month ago, it's been a
   struggle for me to hit the bedtime and awake times consistently. Life
   events, stress..." Dec 2021: "I slipped a little bit... because I'm staying
   overnight at my family's."
5. **Illness → months-long drift → reset needed.** Strep (Aug 2019), colds
   (Nov 2019), health scare (early 2020). The sick weeks weren't the problem;
   the failure to snap back was.
6. **Late-night worry when worry time was skipped.** The worry-time-before-
   dinner rule existed because his anxiety otherwise showed up at bedtime
   (insomnia + anxiety were the joint picture in treatment).

## What demonstrably worked for Ori

- **A named coach who checks.** The morning confirmation emails and spot
  checks; "somehow telling you makes it more real."
- **Two alarms, one across the room.** "Having 2 alarms is clutch... I woke up
  in such a stupor that I might've just fallen back in bed."
- **Earned 15-minute bumps** as visible reward tied to efficiency numbers.
- **The diary habit** — he maintained it for years unprompted.
- **Her voice**: warm, telegraphic, rule-backed, never scolding. "Hang in
  there." "Keep working those tools." "Good! It will get easier as your body
  adjusts! Stick with this!" "We will punch out this middle night wake time
  stuff in short order if you stick with the prescription!" "You are doing
  great in treatment... This is a long game."
